🧠 Various Mental Health Issues & Their Outcomes

Anxiety

Anxiety involves excessive fear, worry, and behavioral disturbances. It can interfere with daily activities and relationships.

  • Restlessness or feeling on edge
  • Rapid heartbeat
  • Trouble concentrating
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Constant worry or fear
Solution: Practice deep breathing, limit stimulants, and engage in CBT or exposure therapy with a trained professional.

Learn More

Depression

More than feeling sad — it can cause a persistent loss of interest, hopelessness, and even physical pain.

  • Persistent sadness or emptiness
  • Loss of interest in daily activities
  • Fatigue or low energy
  • Changes in appetite or weight
  • Thoughts of self-harm or death
Solution: Seek talk therapy, get regular exercise, maintain routines, and consider antidepressants with guidance.

Learn More

PTSD

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder stems from traumatic events and includes flashbacks, nightmares, and emotional numbness.

  • Intrusive memories or flashbacks
  • Nightmares or sleep issues
  • Emotional detachment
  • Hypervigilance
  • Avoidance of reminders
Solution: Supportive therapy, EMDR, grounding techniques, and community support groups.

Learn More

OCD

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder includes intrusive thoughts (obsessions) and repetitive behaviors (compulsions).

  • Unwanted, repeated thoughts
  • Compulsive hand-washing or checking
  • Need for symmetry or order
  • Anxiety if rituals aren't performed
  • Difficulty focusing due to obsessions
Solution: Exposure Response Prevention (ERP) therapy, SSRIs, and mindfulness-based CBT.

Learn More

ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder is marked by impulsivity, hyperactivity, and difficulty focusing or sitting still.

  • Inattention or forgetfulness
  • Impulsive actions
  • Hyperactivity or fidgeting
  • Trouble staying organized
  • Interrupting others or blurting out
Solution: Use planners, break tasks into chunks, behavior therapy, and stimulant medications under guidance.

Learn More

Eating Disorders

Includes anorexia, bulimia, and binge-eating. These are serious illnesses affecting physical and mental health.

  • Preoccupation with weight or body image
  • Extreme food restriction or bingeing
  • Purging through vomiting or laxatives
  • Distorted body perception
  • Social withdrawal or shame about eating
Solution: Nutritional counseling, family therapy, and working with an eating disorder specialist.

Learn More

🌿 Healthy Practices, Eating Habits, and Exercises

  • Practice mindfulness meditation or guided relaxation daily.
  • Stay physically active – aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate activity most days.
  • Eat balanced meals rich in fruits, vegetables, omega-3, and fiber.
  • Stay hydrated and limit caffeine and sugar.
  • Sleep at least 7–9 hours daily with a consistent bedtime routine.
  • Maintain a gratitude journal to reflect on daily positives.
  • Engage in hobbies, creative outlets, or activities that bring joy.
  • Connect with loved ones or support groups regularly.

Learn More

🚫 Things to Avoid

  • Overconsumption of alcohol or recreational drugs.
  • Suppressing emotions instead of expressing them healthily.
  • Spending excessive time on social media.
  • Neglecting your needs for the sake of productivity.
  • Negative self-talk and toxic relationships.
